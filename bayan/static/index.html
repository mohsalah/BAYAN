From a research topic to a proposal

BAYAN searches several scholarly libraries at once and brings the results into one place. Use a research area to find related work, read abstracts, choose the papers that matter, export them, identify candidate research gaps and develop 1–5 research proposals.

  1. Define your area. Type your research area. Choose libraries, reference types, languages and years, then click Search.
  2. Read and choose. Open an abstract with Show abstract, follow the source link, and tick the papers you want to keep.
  3. Export. Download the ticked papers (or all results) as an Excel workbook or a CSV file.
  4. Find research gaps. Choose how many gaps you want (1–5) and click Find research gaps. Copy the evidence prompt into an AI assistant and submit it.
  5. Develop proposals. Paste the gap(s) you want to pursue, choose 1–5 proposals, and click Create proposals. Submit the new prompt the same way.

The gap and proposal buttons prepare an evidence prompt for you to submit; BAYAN does not run the analysis automatically yet. Evidence is limited to titles, metadata and abstracts. Candidate gaps and novelty claims need checking against full texts and a wider literature search. Hover over any button for help.

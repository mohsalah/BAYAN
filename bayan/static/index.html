From a research topic to a proposal

BAYAN searches several scholarly libraries at once and brings the results into one place. Use a research area to find related work, read abstracts, choose the papers that matter, export them, identify candidate research gaps and develop 1–5 research proposals.

  1. Define your area. Type your research area. Choose libraries, reference types, languages and years, then click Search.
  2. Read and choose. Open an abstract with Show abstract, follow the source link, and tick the papers you want to keep.
  3. Export. Download the ticked papers (or all results) as an Excel workbook or a CSV file.
  4. Find research gaps. Choose how many gaps you want (1–5) and click Find research gaps. Copy the evidence prompt into an AI assistant and submit it.
  5. Develop proposals. Paste the gap(s) you want to pursue, choose 1–5 proposals, and click Create proposals. Submit the new prompt the same way.

The gap and proposal buttons prepare an evidence prompt for you to submit; BAYAN does not run the analysis automatically yet. Evidence is limited to titles, metadata and abstracts. Candidate gaps and novelty claims need checking against full texts and a wider literature search. Hover over any button for help.

Keywords for each search language

Each language is searched with its own words. They start as a copy of the research area; edit them to use the field's real terms in that language. A library that cannot filter by language searches the research area once.

Manuals are not found by search; they come from uploads or links in a later version. A library that cannot apply one of your filters says so in the notes under the results.

    Suggest ideas and draft proposals in BAYAN

    BAYAN runs a SciPIP-style pipeline on this run: key terms from your text, an optional brainstorm, papers retrieved from this run (ticked ones first), one inspiration per paper, ideas that cite papers, and a proposal draft per idea. Every citation is checked against the retrieved papers; drafts are unverified candidates.

    Translator

    Translate text, a paper's title and abstract, or pages of a PDF you uploaded into English or Arabic. Every result is a machine translation shown beside the original and is never an evidence quote. Numbers, units, equations, nuclide and chemical names, DOIs and citations are kept unchanged.

    My glossary

    Term pairs are private to your account and are applied to every translation with the same two languages.

    Membership access

    An email address alone gives no access to paid content. Here you can store your own publisher API keys (encrypted on the server, never shown again) and an institutional proxy link. No connector uses these keys yet, so saving a key does not change any search or download today. BAYAN never logs in for you, never fetches through your proxy and never stores a library password.
